Why this site exists: mostly so I remember what I was thinking six months from now.

I don't keep a diary and I don't post much anywhere else, so the failure mode is always the same — something interesting happens, I mean to write it down, and three months later I have a vague feeling but no detail. This is an attempt to fix that, in public, without much ceremony.
